# Retail Leadership Simulation - Content Review Pack

A plain-English extract of everything currently in the simulation, for SME review.
Use it to decide what to **keep**, **lose**, or **add**.

Cadence: **5 shifts x 5 minutes**. Flow: lobby -> briefing -> shift (x5) -> debrief.
Each shift auto-triggers one disruption at the 1-minute mark (facilitator can also trigger early).

_Confidence multiplier is deliberately left out of this pack - assume it applies to all major decisions._

---

## 1. Metrics

### Visible KPIs (shown live to teams)

| KPI | What it measures | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Sales Performance | Commercial output | Higher is better |
| Shrinkage / Loss | Stock loss / waste | **Lower is better** |
| Customer Experience | Service and satisfaction | Higher is better |
| Team Engagement | Morale and buy-in | Higher is better |
| Operational Execution | Getting the basics done | Higher is better |

### Hidden drivers (revealed only after a shift resolves, and on facilitator coaching cards)

| Driver | What it measures | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Safety risk | Exposure to safety/loss incidents | **Lower is better** |
| Team trust | Trust in the leader | Higher is better |
| Capability | Team skill growth | Higher is better |
| Leadership consistency | Predictability / fairness of leadership | Higher is better |

Every team also carries **16 weeks of pre-session baseline history** on each metric, so shift 1 already has trend context.

## 2. Decision inputs

Teams complete these each shift, grouped into tabs.

| Tab | Input | Options | Required? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Focus | **Priority focus** | Safety / Loss, People / Team, Customer, Commercial | Yes |
| Focus | **Action approach** | Apply standard process, Adapt locally, Escalate, Reallocate resource | Yes |
| Team | **Leadership style** | Directive, Collaborative, Coaching, Delegate | Yes |
| Team | **Resource allocation** | 4 sliders - Shop floor, Backroom, Customer service, Problem resolution (must total 100%) | Yes |
| Issue | **Primary issue** | Pick one of the 3 active issues this shift | Optional |
| People | **Response to a direct report** | 4 responses per moment (Directive / Coaching / Delegate / Collaborative) | Required when a moment is present |
| Confidence | **Confidence level** | Cautious / Measured / Confident | (Out of scope for this review) |

### How each input broadly moves the numbers

**Priority focus**
- Safety / Loss: cuts shrinkage and safety risk, small ops gain, slight sales dip
- People / Team: lifts engagement, trust and capability, slight sales dip
- Customer: lifts customer, sales and trust
- Commercial: lifts sales, but dents customer, engagement and trust

**Action approach**
- Standard process: strong ops and consistency, weaker customer/capability
- Adapt locally: strong customer, engagement and capability, weaker ops and consistency
- Escalate: erodes trust, engagement and capability (a "pass it up" move)
- Reallocate: broad small gains across ops, engagement, customer and capability

**Leadership style**
- Directive: ops and consistency up, engagement/trust/capability down
- Collaborative: trust and engagement up, slight ops dip
- Coaching: strongest capability and trust builder, slight sales dip
- Delegate: mild engagement/capability, consistency down

**Resource allocation** - rewards balanced spread; heavy skew (>60% gap) hurts engagement and trust, very even spread gives a small engagement bonus. Shop floor feeds sales, backroom feeds ops, customer service feeds customer + capability, problem resolution cuts shrinkage and safety risk.

**Primary issue choice** - picking an issue that matches your priority gives a real bonus (bigger for higher-severity issues); a mismatch or picking none costs leadership consistency.

**People moment** - see section 6. Not responding at all is heavily penalised.

## 7. Structural caps (design constraints)

- Active issues: **exactly 3** per shift
- Alerts: up to **2** operational/head-office, plus optional 1 disruption (**3 visible max**)
- One people moment per shift where present
- One disruption per shift

These caps drive the left-column layout. Adding more items to a bank increases variety across repeat runs but does **not** increase how many appear per shift.
